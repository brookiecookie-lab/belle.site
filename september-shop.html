Why these six

Merchandising, not catalog dumping.

This page is built from the current LIST cohort because revenue does not need to wait for every new candidate to finish taxonomy and market review.

  • Modern Prep / Intellectual Woman: shirt + trench vest + burgundy corduroy create an immediate outfit system.
  • One-and-done: the jumpsuit solves the “I need to be dressed in five minutes” job.
  • Wear together / separately: the satin two-piece supports higher perceived outfit value without requiring a bundle discount.
  • Statement lane: the patchwork-print shirt dress adds color and visual interest without misrepresenting the supplier’s polyester composition as denim.
  • Machine-readable alongside editorial: each card carries conventional product nouns, price, colors, sizes, material evidence, shipping evidence, product key, and World labels.
