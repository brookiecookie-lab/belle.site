# FASHION EDITORIAL BOOKS ↔ THE PAGES WE KEEP BOUNDARY DOCTRINE 001

Date: 2026-09-08
State: FROZEN / CANONICAL PRODUCT-BOUNDARY DOCTRINE

## Governing distinction

The fashion editorial site does NOT contain a reduced, "Lite," abbreviated, or mini version of **The Pages We Keep**.

There is no product called:
- The Pages We Keep Lite
- Marginalia Society
- The Words We Keep

The canonical standalone reading product is:

**THE PAGES WE KEEP**

It is a separate, deeper domain/product.

The fashion editorial instead contains a deliberately small **Books** editorial lane.

---

## 1. Fashion editorial Books lane

Purpose:
Use books as editorial and cultural objects that deepen the fashion publication without turning the fashion site into a generalized reading platform.

Cadence:
- **1–2 rotating books per month**
- plus a **small evergreen shelf**

Recommended evergreen scale:
- approximately 3–5 titles at a time
- intentionally edited rather than comprehensive

This is a shelf, not a library.

### Appropriate subject lanes

Books may be directly about fashion, or adjacent when the connection is editorially strong:

- fashion
- dress history
- designers
- personal style
- costume
- textiles
- craft
- photography
- image-making
- beauty
- fragrance
- women
- biography / memoir
- art
- film / television / visual culture
- cultural history
- social history
- literature when it materially illuminates a fashion/editorial World
- other titles tied to a current Journal story, World, archetype, or cultural moment

### Monthly book treatment

Each rotating book should remain lightweight enough for the fashion site.

A book card or short feature may include:
- cover / bibliographic identification
- why we picked it
- why it matters now
- the fashion / style / culture connection
- what to notice
- who it is for
- related Journal story, World, archetype, designer, object, beauty lane, or fashion moment
- commerce / affiliate link when lawful, accurate, disclosed, and available
- optional pointer to a deeper Pages We Keep treatment only when one actually exists

The fashion editorial must not pretend that a short recommendation is a full Pages We Keep treatment.

---

## 2. Evergreen shelf

The evergreen shelf is a small permanent or semi-permanent group of foundational books.

It should:
- remain tightly edited
- change slowly
- privilege genuinely useful or culturally important titles
- avoid becoming a giant undifferentiated reading list
- support the publication's fashion + culture identity

Evergreen does not mean permanent forever.
A title can rotate out when the editorial thesis changes.

---

## 3. The Pages We Keep

**The Pages We Keep** is a standalone reading/intellectual product with its own domain and deeper architecture.

The currently supplied library-wide doctrine governs it:

> DON'T JUST ASK THE READER TO FIND THE CONTEXT. PUT THE EVIDENCE ON THE PAGE.

> THE REVOLUTION OUTSIDE; THE REVOLUTION INSIDE.

> YOU CANNOT FULLY UNDERSTAND WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE A STORY UNTIL YOU UNDERSTAND THE WORLD PRESSING AGAINST ITS WALLS.

### The Pages We Keep is allowed to be much deeper

Its architecture may include, where appropriate:
- primary text
- edition/source binding
- historical context
- political context

- material culture

- timelines
- maps
- claims

- changed-mind events
- follow-the-rabbit-hole paths
- contextual documents
- criticism
- contemporaneous records
- visual evidence
- cross-title connections
- external-world events pressing against the story

The fashion editorial Books lane is not required to carry this apparatus.

---

## 4. Cross-link rule

The relationship is:

**FASHION EDITORIAL BOOK → OPTIONAL DEEPER THE PAGES WE KEEP EXPERIENCE**

not:

**FASHION EDITORIAL = THE PAGES WE KEEP**

A fashion-site book can later receive a full standalone Pages We Keep treatment.

When that happens, the fashion editorial may link to it with a clear deeper-reading callout.

The fashion site should not duplicate the deeper product.

---

## 5. Commerce boundary

The fashion editorial can monetize books through lawful and clearly disclosed commerce where appropriate, including:
- affiliate links
- bookseller links
- curated book pairings
- editorial bundles
- future owned objects or companion products when rights-cleared

The Pages We Keep monetization architecture remains separate and can be much broader/deeper.

---

## 6. Current operational rule

For the fashion editorial launch:

**BOOKS = SMALL EDITORIAL SHELF**

Initial target:
- 1–2 featured books per month
- 3–5 evergreen titles maximum

Do not put The Pages We Keep itself inside the fashion site's Digital Products grid as though it is a fashion-site product.

Do not create or use a "Lite" naming variant.

Do not create a fashion-site waitlist that implies the full Pages We Keep product lives there.

The fashion site may later contain a restrained external link to the standalone Pages We Keep domain once that domain is ready.

**Fashion editorial:** small, highly edited Books shelf.

**The Pages We Keep:** standalone deep reading system and domain.

**The fashion site recommends books. The Pages We Keep builds the rabbit hole.**
